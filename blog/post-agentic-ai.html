🔁 From Prompt Chaining to Autonomy: Agentic AI Is Here 🤖

We've come a long way from AI models that required constant nudging through back-and-forth prompts. With Agentic AI, we're now seeing systems capable of autonomously executing tasks, learning, adapting, and even collaborating across tools and workflows.

Whether it's:
✉️ Drafting and replying to customer emails
👩‍💻 Or potentially writing production-grade code on request...

...these agentic systems are blurring the lines between assistant and doer. 🚀

The implications?
✅ Increased productivity
⚠️ Reduced human dependency in certain workflows
🧠 More time for higher-order thinking
